Nursing school

A study app for nursing students that turns your notes into short videos

Short answer: LearnScroll turns your own nursing lecture slides, PDFs and notes (pharmacology, med-surg, fundamentals, maternity, peds) into a feed of short TikTok-style study videos with a quiz every 10 videos and spaced repetition. It works from your course material; it is not an NCLEX question bank.

Where it fits in nursing school

Nursing content is a lot of lists and rules to hold at once: drug classes and their suffixes, lab value ranges, ABG steps, priority frameworks, which insulin peaks when. Short videos with one rule each, repeated over days, suit that kind of material.

Use LearnScroll for the course content you need to remember. Keep using an NCLEX-style question bank for practice questions; LearnScroll does not replace it.

Good things to upload

  • Pharmacology lecture slides (drug classes, suffixes, antidotes, side effects)
  • Lab values and electrolyte handouts
  • Med-surg unit notes (heart failure, DKA vs HHS, SIADH vs DI)
  • Your own handwritten summaries, as photos

A word on accuracy

Videos are made from your material, but AI can still make mistakes. Check doses, ranges and anything clinical against your textbook or lecturer.

LearnScroll in brief

  • iPhone app (runs on iPad), iOS 16.4 or later. No Android app yet.
  • Inputs: PDF (up to 20 MB), PowerPoint (.pptx), Word (.docx), text, photos of printed or handwritten notes, or a typed topic.
  • Output: a vertical feed of short captioned study videos, one key idea each, made from your own material, usually ready in 1 to 3 minutes.

Questions

Yes, as PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF up to 20 MB, or as photos of printed pages.
