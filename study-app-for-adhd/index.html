ADHD and studying

A study app for people with ADHD who can scroll but can't read the textbook

Short answer: LearnScroll is an iPhone app that turns your own PDFs, lecture slides and notes into a feed of short, TikTok-style study videos, one idea per video, with a quick quiz every 10 videos. It does not ask you to sit still with a 40-page chapter; it gives you the same swipe you already do, made from what is on your exam. It is a study tool, not a treatment for ADHD.

Why a feed instead of a textbook

Many students with ADHD describe the same loop: open the PDF, read a paragraph, open TikTok, lose an hour. Willpower apps and timers try to stop the scrolling. LearnScroll keeps the scrolling and changes what is in the feed.

Each video is a few seconds of bold captions over moving footage, about one key idea long. When attention drifts, the next swipe is a new, short thing, not the middle of a dense page.

What it does in practice

  • You upload the material you actually need to learn: lecture slides, a chapter PDF, a photo of handwritten notes, or just a topic.
  • AI splits it into key ideas and makes one short vertical video for each, usually in 1 to 3 minutes.
  • Every 10 videos a quick multiple-choice question checks what stuck, so it is not only passive watching.
  • Spaced repetition brings older videos back right before you would forget them. Nothing to plan or schedule.

What it is not

  • Not a medical or ADHD treatment app, and not a replacement for support from a doctor or your school's disability service.
  • Not a general video platform: everything in your feed comes from your own material.
  • AI can make mistakes, so check key facts against your notes before an exam.

Questions

Is there a study app for ADHD that feels like TikTok?

Yes. LearnScroll (iPhone) turns your PDFs, slides and notes into a vertical feed of short study videos with quizzes every 10 videos and spaced repetition, so studying uses the same swipe habit as TikTok.

Does LearnScroll treat ADHD?

No. It is a study tool that changes the format of your material into short videos. It is not a medical product.

What can I upload?

PDFs up to 20 MB, PowerPoint (.pptx), Word (.docx), text files, photos of printed or handwritten notes, or a typed topic.
