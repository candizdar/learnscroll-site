GCSE and A-level revision

A revision app that turns your GCSE and A-level notes into short videos

Short answer: LearnScroll is an iPhone app that turns your own revision notes, class slides and photos of textbook pages into a feed of short TikTok-style revision videos, one key point each, with a quick quiz every 10 videos and spaced repetition. It works from your material, so the feed matches your exam board's content as closely as your notes do.

How students use it for mocks and finals

  • One topic per upload: for example AQA Biology cell transport, or Edexcel History causes of the First World War.
  • Photograph handwritten notes or upload the PDF your teacher shares on Google Classroom or Teams.
  • Scroll a feed for 10 minutes a day in the weeks before mocks; the quiz every 10 videos is active recall without making flashcards.
  • Spaced repetition brings back the videos you saw days ago, right before you would forget them.

Works for most subjects with facts to remember

Biology, chemistry, psychology, history, geography, RE, business and English Literature context all suit short videos well. Maths and physics problem solving needs practice questions; LearnScroll helps with the definitions and rules around them, not the working.

LearnScroll in brief

  • iPhone app (runs on iPad), iOS 16.4 or later. No Android app yet.
  • Inputs: PDF (up to 20 MB), PowerPoint (.pptx), Word (.docx), text, photos of printed or handwritten notes, or a typed topic.
  • Output: a vertical feed of short captioned study videos, one key idea each, made from your own material, usually ready in 1 to 3 minutes.
  • A multiple-choice quiz every 10 videos; spaced repetition brings older videos back.
  • Videos in 16 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Romanian, Hungarian, Czech, Swedish, Azerbaijani, Indonesian and Vietnamese.
  • LearnScroll Pro: $4.99/month or $29.99/year (US prices), 3-day free trial on the yearly plan, up to 12 uploads a month and up to 160 videos per upload. No ads.

Questions

Is there a revision app that turns notes into videos?

Yes. LearnScroll (iPhone) turns your revision notes, slides or photos of pages into a feed of short TikTok-style revision videos with quizzes every 10 videos.

Does it follow my exam board?

It uses your own material, so it follows whatever your notes and slides cover. It does not contain exam board content of its own.

Is it good for maths?

Only for definitions and rules. Maths problem solving needs worked practice questions.

How much does it cost in the UK?

LearnScroll Pro is billed through the App Store in your local currency; in the US it is $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, with a 3-day free trial on the yearly plan.
