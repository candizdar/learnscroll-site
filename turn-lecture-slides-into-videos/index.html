Lecture slides

How to turn lecture slides into short study videos

Short answer: Export or download the slides as a PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF, open LearnScroll on your iPhone, tap + and add the file, then tap Create. In about 1 to 3 minutes you get a feed of short vertical videos, one key idea from the lecture per video, with a quiz every 10 videos.

Why slides work well

Slides already break a lecture into ideas, one per slide or two. LearnScroll keeps that structure: the videos follow what your lecturer covered instead of a generic summary of the topic.

Slides with very little text (just a picture or a heading) give the AI less to work with. If the lecturer's notes are in the speaker notes or a separate handout, upload that PDF as well.

Step by step

  • Download the slides from your course page (Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, Google Classroom) as .pptx or PDF. Keep PDFs under 20 MB; split very long decks.
  • In LearnScroll tap +, choose the file from Files, and pick the language or leave it on auto.
  • Tap Create. You can leave the app; a notification tells you when the feed is ready.
  • Scroll the feed in short sessions over several days before the exam; spaced repetition brings older videos back.

One lecture per upload

Pro includes 12 uploads a month and up to 160 videos per upload. One lecture or one week of slides per upload keeps each feed focused on one exam topic.

Questions

Can I turn PowerPoint slides into study videos?

Yes. LearnScroll accepts .pptx and PDF slides and turns them into a feed of short vertical study videos with quizzes, usually in 1 to 3 minutes.

Does it make one long video or many short ones?

Many short ones: one key idea per video, like a TikTok feed, plus a quiz every 10 videos.

Is there a file size limit?

PDFs can be up to 20 MB. Split longer decks into parts.

Which languages?

Videos can be made in 16 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Romanian, Hungarian, Czech, Swedish, Azerbaijani, Indonesian and Vietnamese.
