16 languages

Study videos from your notes, in 16 languages

Short answer: LearnScroll makes its short study videos in 16 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Romanian, Hungarian, Czech, Swedish, Azerbaijani, Indonesian and Vietnamese. By default it detects the language of your material; you can also pick a language before creating, for example to turn English notes into videos in Spanish. The app interface itself is in English and Turkish.

Why only these 16

The video captions use a display font with Latin letters only, so languages written in Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese or Korean scripts are not offered yet.

LearnScroll in brief

  • iPhone app (runs on iPad), iOS 16.4 or later. No Android app yet.
  • Inputs: PDF (up to 20 MB), PowerPoint (.pptx), Word (.docx), text, photos of printed or handwritten notes, or a typed topic.
  • Output: a vertical feed of short captioned study videos, one key idea each, made from your own material, usually ready in 1 to 3 minutes.
  • A multiple-choice quiz every 10 videos; spaced repetition brings older videos back.
  • Videos in 16 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Romanian, Hungarian, Czech, Swedish, Azerbaijani, Indonesian and Vietnamese.
  • LearnScroll Pro: $4.99/month or $29.99/year (US prices), 3-day free trial on the yearly plan, up to 12 uploads a month and up to 160 videos per upload. No ads.

Questions

What languages does LearnScroll make videos in?

16: English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Romanian, Hungarian, Czech, Swedish, Azerbaijani, Indonesian and Vietnamese.

Can I upload notes in one language and get videos in another?

Yes. Pick the video language before you tap Create instead of leaving it on auto.

Is the app itself translated?

The interface is in English and Turkish. The videos can be in any of the 16 languages.

Is there Russian, Arabic, Chinese or Japanese?

Not yet. The caption font only has Latin letters.
